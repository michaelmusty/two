## 1. Compute the combined correction through a small level

Retain D104's stable map f:S->C and a ramification point Q above P.
Its intrinsic integral derivative is

    D_Q(df): W_C -> W_S^2.

Let L(Q) be the canonical arithmetic Hodge fibre on S. D104 proved
an integral inclusion W_S -> L(Q), an isometry at infinity, and

    c_L=h_L(Q)-h(W_S)>=0,
    c_ram=finite_zero_degree(D_Q(df))>=0,
    H_CM=2*h_L(Q)+log(2)-2*c_L-c_ram.                 (1)

The new calculation determines the dyadic part of the **sum**
2*c_L+c_ram. It does not claim separate values of c_L or c_ram at the
anchor, nor compute any auxiliary level.

Choose a sufficiently small level away from the dyadic prime, keeping
maximal level at that prime. Let g:S'->S be the resulting cover and
Q' a lift of Q. The generic map g is unramified at Q': all seventeen
points have trivial stabilizer on the norm-one curve, as proved in D112.
[Yuan--Zhang, Theorems 4.5 and 4.7](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v187-n2-p04-p.pdf)
identify the Hodge line with the local dualizing line on this small-level
model and make the identification compatible with level pullback. These
structural results permit the common CM/quaternion ramification here;
their separate CM-height Theorems 1.6/1.7 are not being applied.

The torsion-free uniformizing subgroup has trivial vertex and edge
stabilizers. Its formal model has the standard Drinfeld node charts;
the rational components have three branches since the residue field of
F_p has two elements. Thus they are stable. The nodal description and
stabilizer thicknesses are as in
[Nekovar, section 1.5.3](https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-2011-077-6),
using D99/D110's actual uniformization. After base change, the dualizing
line remains generated by the logarithmic node differential.

The morphism S'->C extends between stable models by
[Liu--Lorenzini, Proposition 4.4(a)](https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/~qliu/articles/modcove.pdf).
D104's integral-differential argument applies also at nodes. At Q',
where df vanishes, the chain rule gives

    D_(Q')(d(f o g)) = (dg|_(Q'))^2 o D_Q(df).

The terms involving a derivative of dg are multiplied by df(Q)=0.
Consequently the composite W_C -> L(Q)^2 is integral, independent of
the chosen level after clearing Q-line powers, and has finite zero
degree 2*c_L+c_ram. At infinity its norm is 2: in centered coordinates
u=w^2, it sends du to 2*(dw)^2. This recovers (1) while allowing its
finite correction to be computed directly on S'.

## 2. Its exact dyadic content in the existing CM coordinates

Use D112's local coordinate z after its exact matrix S. Each CM point
is z=beta, where beta^2=-(2+b), v_beta(beta)=1 and v_beta(b)=2.
The two standard tree vertices delimit the annulus

    |b| < |z| < 1.

Because z=beta lies inside this annulus, the point specializes to the
standard node. PGL2(F_p)-equivariance transports this chart by every
saved S, including the anchor's chart. On the small-level model the
integral Hodge generator is

    lambda=dz/z.

This is a generator of the relative dualizing line, not an ordinary
smooth differential asserted integral at a node. It remains a generator
after a ramified base extension. Put

    w=(z-beta)/(z+beta),      u=w^2.

Then the exact identities are

    lambda=2*dw/(1-w^2),
    D_lambda=(1-w^2)/2 * d/dw,
    d(u)=w*(1-w^2)*lambda,
    D_lambda(w*(1-w^2))|_(w=0)=1/2.                  (2)

D116 identified the stable integral canonical fibre by actual harmonic
evaluation and canonical global generation. Its generator is a unit
times beta^(m_i)*du, with

    (m_0,...,m_16)=(296,33,...,33).

Lifting the fibre generator to a local section can introduce a unit
depending on u. Its derivative contributes only terms vanishing at w=0,
so (2) shows that the composite derivative coefficient is a unit times
beta^(m_i)/2. Its exact valuation is therefore

    d_i=m_i-16=(280,17,...,17).                      (3)

This uses the small-level Hodge generator directly and includes the
entire finite Hodge/stable correction. No guessed generator on the
large-stabilizer quotient is needed. The number 245 from D116's
evaluation saturation defect is not used as a Hodge or residue index.

As a local control on an ordinary edge, use xy=2+b with involution
(x,y)->(-y,-x). The invariant t=x-y satisfies

    D_(dx/x)(x+y)|_(x=beta)=2*beta,
    t=2*beta*(1+u)/(1-u),    (dt/du)|_(u=0)=4*beta.

Thus the downstairs generator has valuation 33 and the derivative
has valuation 17, agreeing with (3). The full argument for all points

## 4. A stronger Faltings bound from the known graph

Apply [de Jong--Shokrieh, Theorems A/B and equation (1.11)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.02637)
to the principally polarized Jacobian of C. Dropping the nonnegative
theta-height, archimedean and other finite terms gives

    h_Fal(C) >= -g*log(pi*sqrt(2))
                + normalized dyadic tropical moment.           (6)

Their norm in (12.1) is i^(g^2)/2^g times the top-form integral. It
equals the determinant of D103's curve inner product (i/2)*integral,
by the period-matrix/Riemann bilinear relation. The integral Hodge
lattices of the stable curve and its Jacobian agree. Thus no extra
log(pi) shift is inserted into (6).

The graph G and its metric are the frozen D99 stable graph: thirty
vertices, forty-five edges, genus sixteen, and total length 76 in
v_b units. The edge lengths are stabilizer thicknesses, including sums
along contracted subdivision chains. The formula

    I(Jac(G))=length(G)/8-tau(G)/2

is [de Jong--Shokrieh, Tropical moments, Theorem 11.4](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/7D168E767FECB100AF1FCF3EA6EF81F9/S0008414X22000220a.pdf/tropical-moments-of-tropical-jacobians.pdf).
Their Theorem 11.2 computes tau from effective resistances:

    tau = sum_(e={a,b}) [ ell_e*(1-r(a,b)/ell_e)^2/12
                         +(r(a,q)-r(b,q))^2/(4*ell_e) ].

Source 453 computes a grounded weighted-Laplacian inverse over Q and
obtains

    tau(G)=67889/27075,
    I(Jac(G))=223268/27075.

Every choice of the thirty base vertices q gives the same answer.
Interval, circle and three-edge-banana controls give the expected
moments 0, 1/4 and 5/18. Review 455 checks the inverse by multiplication
and independently integrates the resistance derivatives by the exact
Simpson rule on each edge, for all thirty basepoints.

Since v_b(2)=8, the normalized dyadic contribution in (6) is

    theta*log(2),      theta=I(Jac(G))/8=55817/54150.

This scaling can also be checked after base change to H: lengths
double, there are seventeen primes, and 17*2/272=1/8. The resulting
lower bound is

    h_Fal(C) >= -16*log(pi)-8*log(2)+theta*log(2)
               > -23.146371.                       (7)

Only the existing reduction graph is used; there is no new graph,
level, census or Hecke computation.

There is also a bookkeeping limitation on common-content proposals.
If a section in an arithmetic line A is known to vanish on an effective
vertical divisor Z, viewing the same section in A(-Z) subtracts deg(Z)
both from the line degree and from its already-counted finite zeros.
The exact-zero inequality is unchanged. Thus content already counted
in the seventeen local jet valuations cannot be subtracted a second
time as a new height gain. A new construction must improve a norm,
an uncounted finite contribution, or the underlying arithmetic line.

construction of the CM residue frame, using D102's principal-parts

The current stable evaluation defect 245 does not establish these
