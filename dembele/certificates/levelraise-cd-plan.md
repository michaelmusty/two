# The level-raising + Cerednik–Drinfeld period route: plan of record

Date: 2026-08-13. Status: **the sole live route** to the explicit polynomial. This
document supersedes the "hard stop" of `idea-exhaustion.md` by opening its one
door labeled "Level-raising + CD at an auxiliary prime — speculative".

## The route in one paragraph

Find an auxiliary prime `q₀` of `F = Q(ζ₃₂)⁺` with `ā_{q₀} = 0` in `F₂₅₆` (the
mod-2 level-raising congruence: since `Nq₀` is odd, `a ≡ ±(Nq₀+1) mod λ` is
exactly `ā = 0`). Then a newform `g` of level `q₀`, Steinberg at `q₀`, is
congruent to Dembélé's `f` mod `λ`, so `ρ̄_g ≅ ρ̄_f` — the **only-at-2 field is
unchanged** even though `g`'s abelian variety `B_g` is bad at `q₀`. The parity
count `7 infinite places + q₀ = 8` (even) now admits an indefinite quaternion
algebra, i.e. a Shimura curve `X/F` with `g ⊂ Jac(X)`. Because `q₀` divides the
discriminant, Cerednik–Drinfeld uniformizes `X` **q₀-adically** through the
*definite* algebra ramified at all 8 infinite places — Dembélé's own algebra,
whose Brandt machinery this directory already reproduces. Compute the q₀-adic
period lattice `Λ` of the 16-dimensional `g`-isotypic piece by
Greenberg/Guitart–Masdeu-style integration on the Bruhat–Tits tree; then
`B_g ≅ (K_{q₀}^×)^{16}/Λ` and the λ-torsion field is generated by **square
roots of lattice data** — recognized algebraically (p-adic LLL, equivariant
gauges as validated in `../rigidity/`), certified by the ready Eisenstein back
end (`eisenstein-prototype.md`), Frobenius data at split primes, and a final
`GaloisGroup`-style check. Output: the degree-257 polynomial over `Q(√2)`.

## Why the archimedean version is dead

`|ζ_F(−1)| = 5820` gives, via Shimizu, hyperbolic area `⇒ 2g−2 ≈ 91·(Nq₀−1)`
for `X`: genus ≥ 1365 even at a fantasy `Nq₀ = 31`, realistically 10⁴–10⁵.
No fundamental-domain computation runs at that scale. The CD/p-adic route has
**no genus wall**: its complexity is the quotient graph of the Bruhat–Tits tree
(≈ Brandt data at level `q₀`, dimension ≈ 58·(Nq₀+1)) and q₀-adic linear
algebra.

## Prior art and the scale gap

The methodology is established and partially Magma-based:

- Teitelbaum (1980s–90s): p-adic periods of Shimura curves via CD, small disc over Q.
- M. Greenberg (2006–09): numerical p-adic integration on the BT-tree (Heegner
  points over Q) — the "G" of the Greenberg–Voight shorthand.
- Guitart–Masdeu (JLMS 2014): overconvergent cohomology makes the integration
  efficient. Guitart–Masdeu–Şengün (J. Algebra 2016): **p-adic periods →
  algebraically recognized curve equations** — our endgame pattern, published,
  for modular elliptic curves. PLMS: number fields of arbitrary signature.
- `darmonpoints` (Masdeu; Sage+Magma hybrid, maintained): implements the whole
  stack; calls Magma for the quaternionic group presentations (Voight
  machinery, same as our toolchain).

Scale frontier vs our demand: base field degree ≤ 3–4 vs **8** (disc 2³¹);
Hecke field degree ≤ 2 vs **16** (residue `F₂₅₆`); uniformizing prime small vs
`Nq₀` possibly in the hundreds. Two structural breaks in our favor: (i) we need
**2-torsion, not equations** — for a multiplicatively uniformized variety that
is square roots of period-lattice generators, far below theta-level difficulty;
(ii) the BT-tree quotient graph *is* definite-side ideal-class/Brandt data we
already compute.

## Contingencies

- **Gate-1 window dry** (no hit at norm ≲ 1000): accept a larger `q₀` (costs
  scale linearly downstream), or optimize the eigenvalue computation, or scan
  degree-2 primes (norm `q²`) which were included but are sparse.
- **Gate-3 congruence absent** at the found `q₀`: continue the scan; the
  congruence is expected (level-raising) but is verified, not assumed.
- **Gate-4 blowup**: the fallback is a bespoke implementation against the
  Brandt data rather than adapting `darmonpoints`; the tree quotient and Hecke
  action are already in our hands, and only the overconvergent lifting is new
  code.
