## 0. Verdict in four sentences

1. For **Dembélé's field itself** (image `SL₂(F₂₅₆)² ⋊ C₈`), no closed door reopens on
   re-reading: every constructive route was killed by a *measured* number (§1), and the
   two that were killed by heuristics (auxiliary-prime lottery, `d_g` size bias) are not
   worth reopening at their price.
2. But the project has been sitting on a **second nonsolvable representation ramified only
   at 2** without noticing: the level-`𝔭` Steinberg newforms over `F = Q(ζ₃₂)⁺` have
   residue field **`F₁₆`** (D36 read this as "not `F₂₅₆`" and moved on). By the standard
   Dickson argument (§2.2) each of these mod-2 systems has image `SL₂(F₁₆)`: a
   nonsolvable extension of `F`, hence of `Q`, ramified only at 2, with Galois group of
   order `~10⁴·8` instead of `~10¹⁴·8`.
3. Its carriers are **4-dimensional** abelian varieties with **multiplicative reduction at
   the norm-2 prime** — precisely the configuration every killed route needed
   (`d_g = 4` not `> 16`; positions 174 not 10⁵; the genus-4 / 17-neighbour Eisenstein
   back end is *already validated* on exactly this shape). The one link never run at any
   dimension (`periods → 2-torsion → recognition`) can be run here at its cheapest.
4. **Recommendation:** pursue the `F₁₆` field as the new explicit target — it answers the
   thesis question (an *explicit* nonsolvable field ramified only at 2) with a degree-17
   polynomial over `F`, and everything learned (heights, kernel constants, recognition)
   prices Dembélé's field afterwards. Details and the phased plan in §4.

## 2. The gap: `F₁₆` Steinberg systems at level `𝔭`

### 2.1 What was computed and how it was read

`57_levelraise_p2.m` (D36): the `𝔭`-new part of the level-`𝔭` Brandt module has
dimension 40, five rational Hecke orbits of degrees `4, 4, 4, 4, 24`, and

    charpoly(T_31) mod 2 = (t⁴+t+1)⁵ · (t⁴+t³+t²+t+1)⁵ ,    U_𝔭 : (t−1)¹⁶ (t+1)²⁴ .

It was read as: "residue field `F₁₆`, not `F₂₅₆`, so `ρ̄_f` cannot occur." Correct — and
beside the point. Each irreducible quartic factor is the minimal polynomial of `ā_31` for a
mod-2 eigensystem of a form that is Steinberg at `𝔭` and unramified elsewhere. Its Galois
representation `ρ̄_g : G_F → GL₂(F̄₂)` is unramified outside `𝔭`, has `det = 1`, and trace
field `F₁₆`.

### 2.2 Why the image is `SL₂(F₁₆)` (argument; certificate in §2.5)

- *Irreducible.* If `ρ̄_g^{ss} = χ₁ ⊕ χ₂`, the `χ_i` are characters of `G_F` unramified
  outside `𝔭` with values in `F̄₂^×` (odd order). They factor through ray class groups
  `Cl_{𝔭^k}(F) = (O_F/𝔭^k)^×/⟨units⟩`, which are 2-groups (`h(F) = 1`, residue field
  `F₂`). So `χ₁ = χ₂ = 1` and `ā_𝔮 = 0` for every `𝔮 ∤ 2` — contradicting `ā_31 ≠ 0`.
  The same argument over `F̄₂` excludes a sum of two conjugate characters.
- *Not dihedral.* A dihedral `ρ̄_g = Ind_M^F ψ` needs `M/F` quadratic unramified outside
  2 (`M = F(√d)`, `d ∈ ⟨−1, ε₁,…,ε₇, π_𝔭⟩` mod squares: 511 fields) and `ψ` of odd order
  on `G_M` unramified outside 2. Traces at split primes are `ζ + ζ⁻¹`, `ζ = ψ(𝔓)`; for
  `ζ` of order 3 or 5 this lies in `F₂` or `F₄` (`ζ+ζ⁻¹` for `ζ ∈ μ₅` satisfies
  `x²+x+1 = 0`), so trace field `F₁₆` forces order 15, i.e. `15 ∣ h(M)`-type
  characters. Rather than class numbers, the cheap certificate: at any prime `𝔮` inert in
  `M`, a dihedral representation has `ā_𝔮 = 0`; so exhibiting, for each of the 511
  classes `d`, a prime `𝔮` with `ā_𝔮 ≠ 0` and `d` a non-square mod `𝔮` excludes them all.
  The eight primes above 31 plus the prime above 97 give a rank-9 system of Legendre
  functionals on the 9-dimensional `F₂`-space of `d`'s, which is what the job checks.
- *Dickson.* An irreducible, non-dihedral finite subgroup of `SL₂(F̄₂)` is conjugate to
  `SL₂(F_{2^m})`, and its traces generate `F_{2^m}`. Trace field `F₁₆` ⇒ `m = 4` ⇒
  image `SL₂(F₁₆)` (order 4080, simple).

Consequence: `E_g := F̄^{ker ρ̄_g}` is an `SL₂(F₁₆)`-extension of `F` ramified only at
`𝔭`; its Galois closure over `Q` is nonsolvable and ramified only at 2. **This is a second
answer to the thesis question**, with a much smaller group (`SL₂(16)^m ⋊ C₈`, `m ≤ 8`;
the job determines `m` from the `C₈`-action on the residual systems).

Why nobody saw it: Dembélé's level-1 weight-2 search sees only the Serre weight `V₀`
(trivial) at `𝔭`. With residue field `F₂` there are exactly two Serre weights, `V₀` and
`V₁ = St`; a representation whose only weight is `V₁` (très ramifié at `𝔭`, as a
Steinberg form's Tate-type 2-torsion is) is invisible at level 1 and first appears at
level `𝔭`. The level-`𝔭` weight-2 space (dim 154) therefore contains *every* modular
mod-2 representation of `G_F` unramified outside `𝔭`: the `F₂₅₆` pair (Dembélé, old at
`𝔭`) and the `F₁₆` systems (new at `𝔭`). Nothing else is there (D36: those are the only
nonzero mod-2 factors).

### 2.3 What it gives

**(a) A smaller explicit target.** The projective action of `SL₂(F₁₆)` on `P¹(F₁₆)`
gives a degree-**17** polynomial over `F` (degree 136 over `Q`) instead of degree 257
over `Q(√2)`. Its root discriminant is bounded by `2^{(79 + 31·17)/136} ≈ 22` (wild
inertia of order 16, different exponent `≤ 15 + 4·16 = 79`), and Bosman's degree-18–24
analogues over `Q` have `log₁₀ H ≈ 5–15`: the *field* is small.

**(b) The cheapest possible carrier.** A degree-4 orbit `g` gives `A_g/F` of dimension 4
with RM by a quartic field `H_g` in which 2 is inert (`O/λ = F₁₆`), so
`A_g[2] = A_g[λ]` and `F(A_g[2]) = E_g`. Steinberg at `𝔭` ⇒ multiplicative reduction ⇒
Cerednik–Drinfeld uniformization by **Dembélé's definite order on the 3-regular tree**:
the quotient graph *is* the level-`𝔭` Brandt module (155 edges, 2×58 vertices), the
monodromy pairing is the banked stabilizer-order diagonal (D31), the period lattice has
rank 4. Every parameter that killed the `q₀` route flips:

| parameter | at `q₀` (D32/D33) | at `𝔭`, `F₁₆` carrier |
|---|---|---|
| `d_g` | `> 16` (needed 16) | **4** |
| tree positions | `~1.2·10⁵` | **174** |
| uniformizing residue field | `F₂₄₀₁` | `F₂` |
| term count of the invariant | `M^{16}/Δ′` | `M^4/Δ′` |
| neighbours to separate | 257 (untested) | **17 — the validated genus-4 control** |
| heights | `v(q) ~ 10⁴` at norm 31 | §2.5 (Gram diagonal / 2) |

**(c) The never-run link becomes runnable.** `roadmap-reevaluation.md` and
`gate4-dim2-calibration.md` both name `period lattice → 2-torsion → algebraic
recognition` as the one link never executed at any dimension. Here it can be executed
end to end at dimension 4 with a known answer to certify against (Frobenius cycle types
on `P¹(F₁₆)` from `ā_𝔮`, then `GaloisGroup`), and the D34 one-pass lift "applies as is"
at these positions.

**(d) Relation to Dembélé's field.** Not the same field. But (i) it is *an* explicit
nonsolvable field ramified only at 2 — the original question; (ii) it is the only object
in the project on which hole (2) (a recognizable invariant of small height) can be
studied with data instead of extrapolation; (iii) a working norm-2 pipeline is the
strongest possible preparation should a `d_g = 16` auxiliary prime ever appear for `ρ̄_f`.

### 2.4 What could go wrong

- *Dihedral.* Excluded by the certificate if the Legendre rank reaches 9; if a class `d`
  survives, its class number decides (degree-16 field, GRH class group, minutes).
- *Base change.* If a residual system is `τ⁴`-invariant it may descend to `Q(ζ₁₆)⁺`; that
  would make the field *smaller* still (and contradict nothing: the earlier LMFDB sweep
  over `Q(ζ₁₆)⁺` was at the levels LMFDB holds). The job tests `τ^i`-invariance of each
  orbit's charpolys and sweeps 2-power levels over `Q(ζ₁₆)⁺` and `Q(√2)` directly.
- *Heights.* The monodromy Gram matrices (job §5) give `v(q)`; if the entries are
  `~10⁴` as at level 31 the recognition front end is again the problem — but at
  dimension 4 and 174 positions the lift cost at `M ~ 10⁴` is `10²–10³×` below the
  level-31 pricing of D34, and the *field* height (a) is small, so a small-height
  invariant is the thing to design.
- *Sign / splitting.* `U_𝔭 = ±1` decides split vs non-split multiplicative reduction
  (Tate uniformization over `F_𝔭` vs its unramified quadratic extension); either works.

**(c) For `ρ̄_f` itself.** Nothing new. The auxiliary-prime lottery stays a lottery
(`~10⁻³`); the intrinsic obstructions (`ā_𝔭 = ω`, `d_g` size bias, `M_rec`) do not move.
What *does* move is the information content of hole (2): the `F₁₆` pipeline is the first
place where "which invariant has small height" can be measured on a real nonsolvable
2-only field.

**(d) The archimedean route at `𝔭`, reopened.** `gate4-darmonpoints-assessment.md`
priced the Shimura-curve fundamental domain as area `571.4·(Nq₀ − 1)`: `~10⁶` at `q₀`.
At `𝔭` (`N𝔭 − 1 = 1`) the area is **571**, genus 40 — inside the range (10–10³) where
Voight's algorithm has been run, albeit over a degree-8 base field with `disc 2³¹`. Magma's
`FuchsianGroup` for the algebra ramified at `𝔭` and seven real places is a one-line

and the `𝔭`-adic route becomes a cross-check rather than the only path. This was

there.

**(e) Curves with good reduction outside 2 beyond Belyi maps.** Genus 2 is settled
(Jones: no nonsolvable sextic ramified only at 2); hyperelliptic genus 3 by Lesseni's

## 5. Not claimed

- Nothing here is a theorem about `ρ̄_f`. The new field is a *different* representation.
- §2.2 is standard (Dickson; class-field-theoretic vanishing of odd characters); the
  computational certificate is the anti-dihedral test plus the trace-field reading. The
  identification of the mod-2 eigensystems with the Galois representations of the
  corresponding characteristic-zero newforms is the usual Eichler–Shimura / Carayol /
  Taylor construction for Hilbert modular forms of parallel weight 2 (unconditional here:
  the forms are Steinberg at `𝔭`, so the Shimura curve exists — this is *not* the
  Eichler–Shimura-conjectural corner of `csv-paper-adaptation.md`).
- Heights and costs in §2.3(b) are the parameters, not results; §2.5 has the numbers.
